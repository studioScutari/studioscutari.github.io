FAQ

Common questions

Does the game collect my personal data?

No. Aboard: Pirate Conquest has no accounts and no servers of its own; your settings stay on your device. The only third party is Google AdMob, which shows optional ads — see the privacy policy.

Why are there ads?

The game is free. Ads are always optional: you only see one when you choose to watch it for a reward, such as an instant repair or a doubled treasure chest.

Is there a mode for younger players?

Yes. Turn on Kid-friendly mode in Settings › Language. Ads are then requested as suitable for all ages and non-personalised, and the tracking permission is not asked.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 15 or later.
