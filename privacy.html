Privacy Policy - Bosphorus Arcade

Effective date: 17 October 2026

This policy explains what information is handled when you play Bosphorus Arcade (the "Game"), an iOS game developed by Studio Scutari ("we", "us"). If you have any question, write to bilalemregirit@gmail.com.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The Game has no user accounts, no sign-in and no servers of its own. Your game settings (sound, language, controls, display options) are saved only on your device and are deleted when you remove the Game.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game is free and shows optional rewarded ads: an ad is only shown when you choose to watch one (for example to repair your ship instantly or to double a chest reward). These ads are provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • the device's advertising identifier (IDFA) - only if you allow tracking (see section 4);
  • IP address and the approximate (country / city level) location derived from it;
  • device information such as model, operating system version and language;
  • ad interaction data (for example whether an ad was shown, watched or tapped);
  • diagnostic data used to prevent fraud and fix technical problems.

Google uses this information according to its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

3. Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the Game asks for your consent (using Google's consent form) before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the Game under Settings > About > Ad Privacy.

4. App Tracking Transparency (iOS)

On iOS the Game asks for your permission before your advertising identifier can be used for tracking. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads are still shown but they are not personalised using that identifier. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

5. Kid-friendly mode and children

The Game is intended for a general audience. It includes a kid-friendly mode (Settings > Language): when it is on, the tracking permission is not requested and ads are requested as child-directed and suitable for all ages (non-personalised). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, contact us and we will help to have it removed.

6. Sharing and selling

We do not sell or rent any information. Apart from the advertising described in section 2, no information leaves your device because of the Game.

7. Your rights

Because we do not hold any personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For data processed by Google for advertising, you can use your consent choice (section 3), the iOS tracking setting (section 4) and Google's controls at My Ad Center. You may also contact us at any time.

8. Security

Settings stored on your device are protected by the security of your device. Communication between the Game and Google's ad services is encrypted (HTTPS).

9. Changes to this policy

If the Game changes the way it handles information (for example by adding new features or services), we will update this page and its effective date.
