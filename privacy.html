At a glance

  • We don't collect, store or sell your personal information.
  • No accounts, no sign-in — your settings stay on your device.
  • Ads are optional and provided by Google AdMob.
  • You decide about tracking and personalised ads, and can change it any time.

This policy explains what information is handled when you play Aboard: Pirate Conquest (the “Game”), an iOS game made by Studio Scutari (“we”, “us”). Questions are always welcome at studioscutari@gmail.com.

01What we collect

We do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The Game has no user accounts, no sign-in and no servers of its own. Your game settings — sound, language, controls and display options — are saved only on your device and are removed when you delete the Game.

02Advertising

The Game is free and offers optional rewarded ads: an ad is only shown when you choose to watch one, for example to repair your ship instantly or to double a treasure chest. These ads are provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show and measure ads, Google may process:

  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) — only if you allow tracking (see section 04);
  • your IP address and the approximate, city-level location derived from it;
  • device information such as model, operating system version and language;
  • ad interaction data, such as whether an ad was shown, watched or tapped;
  • diagnostic data used to prevent fraud and fix technical problems.

Google handles this information under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

03Consent in Europe

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the Game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads can be shown. You can review or change your choice at any time in the Game: Settings › About › Ad Privacy.

04Tracking on iOS

On iOS the Game asks for your permission before your advertising identifier may be used for tracking. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, ads are still available but are not personalised using that identifier. You can change this at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

05Children

The Game is made for a general audience and includes a kid-friendly mode (Settings › Language). When it is on, the tracking permission is not requested and ads are requested as child-directed, suitable for all ages and non-personalised. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, please contact us and we will help to have it removed.

06Sharing

We do not sell or rent any information. Apart from the advertising described in section 02, no information leaves your device because of the Game.

07Your rights

Because we do not hold personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For data processed by Google for advertising, you can use your consent choice (section 03), the iOS tracking setting (section 04) and Google's controls at My Ad Center. You are always welcome to contact us with any request.

08US state privacy rights

If you live in a US state with a consumer privacy law — such as California (CCPA / CPRA), Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, Texas or Oregon — you have the right to know what personal information is processed, to request its deletion, and to opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information for targeted advertising.

  • We do not sell personal information for money and we do not collect it ourselves.
  • Showing personalised ads through Google AdMob may count as “sharing” under some state laws. You can opt out in the Game under Settings › About › Ad Privacy (shown in states where it applies), and by choosing “Ask App Not to Track” in iOS (section 04).
  • After opting out, ads are still shown but are not personalised.
  • To make any other request, write to studioscutari@gmail.com. We will not treat you differently for using your privacy rights.

09Security

Settings stored on your device are protected by your device's own security. Communication between the Game and Google's ad services is encrypted (HTTPS).

10Changes to this policy

If the Game changes the way it handles information — for example when new features or services are added — we will update this page and its effective date.
